Card Vault User Guide

Complete guide to managing your graded card inventory, tracking prices, and identifying the best cards to buy and sell.

Initial Setup
One-time steps to get Card Vault running

Your App URL

Card Vault lives at: justinnymark.github.io/cardvault/

Bookmark this on every device you use. On iPhone, tap the share button → Add to Home Screen to get an icon like a real app.

Anthropic API Key (First-Time Only)

The first time you open the app, it asks for your Anthropic API key. This is what powers the AI that reads card photos and searches for prices.

1
Go to console.anthropic.com
Sign in with the account you already have.
2
Click API Keys in the left menu
Then click Create Key and give it any name (e.g. "Card Vault").
3
Copy the key
It starts with sk-ant-. Copy the whole thing — you only see it once.
4
Paste it into the Card Vault setup screen
The app tests the connection and saves the key on your device. You never enter it again on that device.
Cost: Scanning 100 card photos costs roughly $0.10. Running a price refresh on 300 cards costs around $0.30–0.50. Your Anthropic account is billed monthly for actual usage — it is not included in your Claude.ai subscription.
Privacy: Your API key is stored only on your device. It is never sent anywhere except directly to Anthropic's servers when the app makes a request.
Dad & Owen Profiles
Two completely separate collections on one app

When you open the app you choose either Dad or Owen. Each profile has its own inventory, its own price data, and its own stats. They are completely siloed — nothing crosses over.

👁 Browsing Each Other's Collection

Both profiles can view each other's collection in read-only mode. From any profile, tap Owen's (or Dad's) in the top navigation. You can search and filter but cannot edit or add cards.

🔄 Switching Profiles

Tap your name badge in the top-right corner of the nav bar to return to the profile selector screen.

Adding Cards
Two methods: photo identification or manual entry

Method 1 — Photo ID (Recommended)

Make sure you are in BUY mode (top-right toggle), then tap + Add Card.

1
Select the 📷 Photo tab
Tap the upload area and select a photo from your camera roll, or take one directly.
2
Tap "Identify Cards"
The AI reads every PSA/BGS/SGC/CGC label in the photo — one card or a whole pile at once. It reads player name, year, brand, set, grading company, grade, cert number, and card attributes (RC, Auto, Patch, serial numbered).
3
Enter purchase info for each card
After identification, each card gets its own purchase price, date, and source fields. Fill in what you paid for each card individually.
4
Tap "Add X Cards"
All identified cards are added to your inventory in one tap.
Photo tips: Lay cards flat with the PSA/BGS label clearly visible. Bright, even lighting works best. One card or 20 at a time — both work fine. If a card isn't identified, try a closer or clearer photo.

Method 2 — Manual Entry

Tap + Add Card → select the 📝 Manual tab. Fill in all fields and tap Add Card. Use this for cards where the photo isn't reading cleanly.

Buying at a Show — Bulk Photo

At a card show, lay all your purchased cards on a flat surface and take one photo of the group. Upload it in Buy mode and the AI identifies all of them at once. Enter the price for each card individually, then tap Add.

My Cards & Filters
Search, filter, and sort your entire collection

Search

The search bar at the top of My Cards searches across player name, year, brand, set, cert number, and sport simultaneously. Just start typing.

Inside the Filters panel, use the Sort By buttons to sort by Player, Year, Brand, Grading Company, Grade, Cost, Last Sale, or Alert status. Tap the same button again to reverse the sort direction (↑ ↓).

Morning Price Refresh
Automated price lookup across eBay sold listings and auction houses

The price refresh searches eBay completed listings, PWCC, and Goldin for recent sold prices on every active card in your inventory. It updates the Last Sale price, calculates your P&L, and sets alert status automatically.

How to Run

From the Home dashboard, tap Run Refresh. A progress bar shows which card is being checked. Each card takes a few seconds — 300 cards takes roughly 5–8 minutes total.

Best time to run: Morning before the market opens, or after a big show when prices may have moved.

Date Protection

If you've manually entered a last sale price (e.g. from Card Ladder), the refresh will not overwrite it unless it finds a sale that is more recent. Your manual entries are always protected from older auto data.

Alert Thresholds

AlertCondition
Urgent SaleLast sale is 25%+ above cost AND gain exceeds $25
Time to SellLast sale is 10–24% above cost AND gain exceeds $25
NormalEverything else
Note on 130point: 130point has bot protection that blocks automated searches. The price refresh uses eBay and auction house data instead. Use the 130point link inside each card's detail view for manual research.
Sell Recommendations
Your ranked list of cards to move — updated in real time

Tap Sell Now in the navigation. This view ranks every card in your inventory by a score that combines ROI percentage, alert status, and margin. It answers the question: "If I'm going to a show tomorrow, which cards do I bring?"

Sections

🟢 Sell Now

Cards with a strong sell or consider signal. Sorted by score — highest ROI and alert status at the top.

⏳ Hold

Cards that are profitable but haven't hit a sell threshold. Market may not be right yet.

🔴 Underwater

Cards where last sale is below your cost. Useful for identifying losses before a show.

❓ No Price Data

Cards that haven't had a price refresh yet, or where no recent sales were found. Run a refresh or enter manually.

Pro tip: Run the Morning Refresh before checking this screen. The recommendations are only as good as the price data behind them.
Selling Cards
Mark individual cards or bulk sells from a photo

Selling a Single Card

Switch to SELL mode using the toggle in the top-right. In My Cards, a Sell button appears on each in-inventory card. Tap it, enter the sale price and date, and tap Confirm. The app shows your profit/loss instantly.

Bulk Sell at a Show

When you sell multiple cards at once at a show:

1
Switch to Sell mode
Use the BUY/SELL toggle in the nav.
2
Tap + Add Card
Upload a photo of all the cards you just sold together.
3
Tap Identify Cards
The AI matches each identified card to your inventory.
4
Enter the sale price and confirm
Check/uncheck which cards were sold (some may not match), enter the total sale price, and tap Mark Sold.
Watchlist
Track cards you're thinking about buying

Tap Watchlist in the navigation to manage your potential buy list.

Adding a Card to Watch

Enter a description of the card in the search field — include year, brand, set, player, and grade for best results. Example: 2023 Topps Chrome Patrick Mahomes PSA 10

Optionally set a Target Buy Price — the app will flag when the last sale hits or drops below your target.

Checking Prices

Tap the 🔄 button on any watchlist item to look up recent sold prices. The app returns:

  • Last Sale Price — most recent completed sale
  • Avg Recent Price — average of last 3–5 sales
  • Lowest Recent Price — floor of the recent market
  • Verdict — Good Buy / Fair Price / Overpriced based on market data

Tap 🔄 Check All to refresh prices on every watchlist item at once.

If a card hits or drops below your target buy price, it's flagged with a green 🎯 AT OR BELOW TARGET banner.
Price Alerts
How alerts work and how to act on them

Alerts appear on the Home dashboard and on each card in inventory. They are recalculated every time a price refresh runs or a price is manually updated.

Urgent Sale — Act now

Last sale is 25% or more above what you paid, and the dollar gain exceeds $25. These cards are at or near a peak — consider selling before the market corrects.

Time to Sell — Watch closely

Last sale is 10–24% above cost with at least $25 gain. Not urgent but worth listing or bringing to your next show.

The $25 minimum gain floor: A $10 card jumping 30% is a $3 gain — not worth your effort. The alert only fires if the actual dollar gain is meaningful. This threshold applies to both alert levels.
Manual Price Updates
Enter prices from Card Ladder or any other source

Since Card Ladder has bot protection that prevents automated access, you can look up prices there yourself and enter them manually.

1
Open the card's detail view
Tap View on any card in My Cards.
2
Tap "View recent sales on 130point"
This opens 130point pre-searched for that exact card. You can also check Card Ladder yourself.
3
Enter the price in "Manual Last Sale Update"
In the same detail view, scroll to the Manual Last Sale Update section. Enter the price and date, then tap Save Last Sale Price.
Date protection: If the morning refresh later finds an eBay sale, it will only overwrite your manual entry if the eBay sale is more recent than the date you entered.
Bulk Price Update
Update last sale prices for many cards at once

From the Home dashboard, tap 📋 Bulk Price Update. Every active card in your inventory appears in a list. Enter the last sale price and date for as many as you want — leave others blank to skip them. Tap Save All Prices when done.

This is the fastest way to catch up after checking Card Ladder — look up 10 cards there, then enter all the prices in one go.

CSV Export
Download your full inventory as a spreadsheet

From the Home dashboard, tap ⬇ Export CSV. A spreadsheet downloads to your device with every card and every field including P&L calculations.

The CSV includes: Player, Year, Brand, Set, Grading Co, Grade, Cert #, RC, Auto, Patch, Serial, Print Run, Purchase Price, Purchase Date, Where Purchased, Last Sale Price, Last Sale Date, Est. Value, P&L, P&L %, Alert Status, Card Status, Notes.

Export regularly as a backup. Your data is stored in your browser's local storage — clearing browser data will delete it. The CSV is your safety net.
130point Research Link
Quick access to full sales history for any card

Open any card's detail view (tap View) and scroll to the bottom. Tap 🔍 View recent sales on 130point. The link opens 130point pre-searched for that exact card — player, year, brand, set, grading company, and grade are all pre-filled.

130point shows sales from eBay, Goldin, Heritage, PWCC, Fanatics, Alt, and other platforms in one view. You'll need to pass their bot-check the first time (one-time per browser session).

Cross-Device Sync (Supabase)
Your inventory syncs across Mac, iPhone, and any device with the link

Card Vault uses Supabase as a free cloud database to keep your inventory identical on every device. When you add a card on your Mac, it instantly appears on your phone — and vice versa.

One-Time Supabase Setup

1
Go to supabase.com → Sign up free
Use GitHub login or email. No credit card required. The free tier is more than enough for 300+ cards.
2
Create a new project named "card-vault"
Choose US East region (closest to Florida). Set a database password and save it. Wait ~2 minutes for provisioning.
3
Run this SQL to create your tables
In your Supabase project, click SQL Editor in the left sidebar, paste the following, and click Run:
create table cards (
  id text primary key,
  profile text not null,
  data jsonb not null,
  updated_at timestamp default now()
);

create table watchlist (
  id text primary key,
  profile text not null,
  data jsonb not null,
  updated_at timestamp default now()
);
4
Get your credentials
Click Settings (gear icon) → API. Copy two things: your Project URL (looks like https://xxxx.supabase.co) and your anon public key (starts with eyJ).
5
Enter both keys in Card Vault setup
Open Card Vault on any device. The setup screen has two sections — one for your Anthropic key and one for Supabase. Enter both and tap Save & Get Started. The app tests both connections before saving.
6
Repeat on every device
Open Card Vault on your phone → enter the same two Supabase keys (and your Anthropic key). From that point forward, all data syncs automatically across every device.
Anthropic key vs Supabase key: The Anthropic key is device-specific (stored locally, never in the cloud). The Supabase keys are the same on every device — they point to your shared database.
What syncs: Dad's inventory, Owen's inventory, Dad's watchlist, Owen's watchlist, and all price/alert data.
What doesn't sync: Your Anthropic API key (stays on each device for security). Last refresh timestamp (cosmetic, per device).

Migrating Existing Data

If you already had cards stored on your Mac before setting up Supabase, those cards are in your Mac's local storage. After completing Supabase setup, your data will push to Supabase automatically the next time you save or add a card. To force a sync immediately, edit any card (even just add a space to Notes and save it).

Important: If you reset your API keys (Settings → Reset All Keys), you'll need to re-enter both your Anthropic key AND your Supabase keys on that device. Your data in Supabase is safe — resetting keys only affects that device's access.
Updating the App
How to apply upgrades Claude provides

When a new version of Card Vault is ready, Claude will provide an updated index.html file (and an updated guide.html if the guide changed). Here's how to deploy it:

1
Download the new file from Claude
In the Claude chat, tap the file attachment and download it to your computer.
2
Go to github.com/justinnymark/cardvault
Sign in to GitHub and open your cardvault repository.
3
Delete the old file
Click index.html (or guide.html) → click the trash/delete icon → click Commit changes.
4
Upload the new file
Click Add file → Upload files → drag in the new file → click Commit changes.
5
Hard refresh your browser
Wait 60 seconds for GitHub to deploy, then press Cmd + Shift + R (Mac) or Ctrl + Shift + R (Windows) to clear the cache.
Important: Your card data is stored in your browser's local storage — it is NOT in the GitHub files. Updating the app never touches your inventory data.

Troubleshooting
Common issues and fixes
🔲 App shows blank white screen

Do a hard refresh: Cmd + Shift + R (Mac) or Ctrl + Shift + R (Windows). If that doesn't fix it, the most recently uploaded file may have a code error — start a new Claude conversation and ask to diagnose.

📷 Photo not identifying cards

Make sure the PSA/BGS/SGC/CGC label is clearly visible and in focus. Try better lighting or a closer shot. If you get an "API Error" message, your Anthropic API key may need to be reset (go to ⚙ Settings in the app).

💰 Price refresh shows blank last sales

Some cards have very few recent sales or the search can't find them. Use the Manual Last Sale Update inside the card's detail view, or use the Bulk Price Update from the Home screen after checking Card Ladder or 130point yourself.

🔑 "Connection failed" on API key entry

Double-check that the key starts with sk-ant- and was copied in full. Make sure your Anthropic account has a valid payment method and available credits at console.anthropic.com.

📱 App looks outdated after upload

GitHub takes up to 60 seconds to deploy. After uploading a new file, wait a minute then do a hard refresh (Cmd + Shift + R). If still outdated, try opening the URL in a private/incognito window.

📱 Cards don't appear on my phone / another device

You need to enter your Supabase credentials on each device. Open Card Vault on the new device → if it shows the setup screen, enter your Anthropic key and both Supabase keys. If it goes straight to the profile selector, go to ⚙ Settings → Reset All Keys → re-enter everything. Your data in Supabase is safe throughout.

🔄 Changes on one device aren't showing on another

Try a hard refresh (Cmd + Shift + R) on the device that seems out of date. Data syncs to Supabase instantly on save, but the browser may be showing a cached version of the page.

💾 My cards disappeared

If Supabase is set up, your data is safe in the cloud — just re-enter your Supabase keys on this device (Settings → Reset All Keys). If Supabase was not configured, data was local only. Export a CSV backup regularly from the Home screen as a safety net.
